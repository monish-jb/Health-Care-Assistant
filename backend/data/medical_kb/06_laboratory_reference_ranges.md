# CLINICAL REFERENCE: LABORATORY REFERENCE RANGES AND VALUES INTERPRETATION

## SECTION 1: COMPLETE BLOOD COUNT (CBC) REFERENCE RANGES
- Hemoglobin (Hb):
  - Adult Male: 13.8 to 17.2 g/dL.
  - Adult Female: 12.1 to 15.1 g/dL.
  - Clinical Significance: Low levels indicate anemia (iron deficiency, chronic disease, bleeding). Severe anemia (< 8.0 g/dL) causes fatigue, tachycardia, and pallor.
- White Blood Cell Count (WBC): 4,500 to 11,000 cells/mcL.
  - Elevated (Leukocytosis > 11,000): Indicates bacterial infection, acute inflammation, or severe stress.
- Platelets: 150,000 to 450,000 /mcL.

## SECTION 2: METABOLIC AND ENDOCRINE REFERENCE RANGES
- Serum Glucose (Fasting): 70 to 99 mg/dL.
- HbA1c: Normal < 5.7%. Prediabetes: 5.7% - 6.4%. Diabetes >= 6.5%.
- Thyroid-Stimulating Hormone (TSH): 0.45 to 4.50 mIU/L.
- Serum Creatinine: 0.7 to 1.3 mg/dL (Male), 0.6 to 1.1 mg/dL (Female). High levels indicate impaired renal clearance.
- Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT): 7 to 56 U/L. Aspartate Aminotransferase (AST): 10 to 40 U/L. Elevated liver transaminases indicate hepatic injury.
